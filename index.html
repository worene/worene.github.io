Our mission

Our goal is simple: support children and families in the Greater Fall River area through outreach, local programs, and community drives.

Our story

Hi, I'm Cassidy. I'm 10. I saw kids in need of help with stuff other than food and water, stuff I could actually help with, and I wanted to do something about it, so I begged my dad until he said yes. That's how Fall River Cares started.

— Cassidy, founder
